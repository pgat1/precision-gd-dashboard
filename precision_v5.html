What the cancel reasons actually mean
The reported reason and the real reason are not the same
Customer repaired door — 53 jobs (49%)
Not customers who fixed it. Customers who waited through their window, got no call from tech or office, and either gave up, found someone else, or had the issue resolve itself. Job history shows zero outbound contact on most of these.
Duplicate Job — 15 jobs (14%)
Pure internal booking error. IAA/Rover books a job, then a CSR books the same job again. Josh Compton logged 5 of these — worth a process check on how calls get handed from Rover to the office.
Customer's schedule — 14 jobs (13%)
Window didn't work for the customer. Many of these had urgent issues (car trapped) but were given the next available slot — 1-3 days out. Customer found a solution before then.
Found someone sooner — 4 jobs ← most expensive
Direct revenue lost to competition. These customers called you first, told you the problem, got a booking, then found a competitor who arrived faster. Every one of these was an urgent job.
